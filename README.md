## 🎮 About the Game

the cabin game is a first-person psychological horror and card-based cooperative game developed and published by Twisted Corridor. It entered Early Access on August 27, 2026, for 1 to 4 players on PC. Players are trapped inside a shifting cabin and must use cards, investigate clues, and survive supernatural threats to escape.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **the cabin game**. This trainer provides quality-of-life and progression controls for the cabin game. It lets players manage the clock, monster pressure, card effects, clue discovery, and survival conditions while exploring player-built cabin layouts in solo or online cooperative sessions.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed way to learn the card system, inspect player-built cabin layouts, practice investigation routes, or reduce the pressure of monster events in solo and cooperative sessions.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Freeze Clock** — Stops the cabin's clock track from advancing while players investigate rooms, study clues, or plan their next card play.
- **Slow Monster Marker** — Reduces the pressure created by the monster marker catching the player marker during a run.
- **Unlimited Lifesaver Cards** — Prevents Lifesaver Card usage from being depleted when players need to push the monster marker away.
- **Disable Time-Skip Effects** — Blocks card effects that advance the run's clock and shortens the pressure caused by sudden time jumps.
- **Event Control** — Reduces or disables disruptive event effects that can interrupt the card-table phase and trigger threats in the cabin.

### 📌 Investigation

- **Reveal Clues** — Reveals investigation clues used to identify the correct escape room.
- **Identify Escape Room** — Displays the room associated with the current escape solution, helping players navigate the procedurally arranged cabin.
- **Safe Investigation Mode** — Combines protection from damage with reduced time and monster pressure for players focused on learning room layouts and clue locations.

### 📌 Survival

- **No Player Damage** — Protects players from damage caused by supernatural encounters and hostile events inside the cabin.
- **No Death** — Prevents fatal outcomes during exploration, allowing the group to continue investigating after dangerous encounters.

### 🚶 Movement

- **Movement Speed** — Adjusts first-person movement speed for faster searches, emergency escapes, or more deliberate exploration.

### 🌍 World

- **Free Cabin Exploration** — Allows players to explore connected rooms and inspect the player-designed cabin without normal clock pressure.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

The cabin is explored from a first-person perspective after rooms are placed through the card system. Use the movement speed option to search connected rooms more quickly or to practice routes between clues and the escape room. Keep the cabin layout in mind because rooms are arranged by players during the run rather than selected from one fixed map.

### Tools & Inventory

The game revolves around dealt cards rather than a traditional equipment inventory. Lifesaver Cards can influence the monster marker, while Room Cards, Progress Cards, Event Cards, and other effects shape the cabin and the current run. Activate unlimited Lifesaver Cards before using one repeatedly, and disable time-skip effects when you want to study card interactions without advancing the clock.

### World & Environment

Players construct the cabin as the session progresses by placing room cards and connecting new areas. The trainer can freeze the clock, reduce monster pressure, reveal clues, and identify the escape room so players can focus on understanding the changing layout. Use free exploration after building a connected cabin to inspect rooms and learn efficient search paths.

### Combat & Weapons

the cabin game is built around psychological horror, investigation, card effects, and survival rather than conventional weapon-based combat. Hostile monsters and events create danger during exploration. No death and no damage options are intended for learning encounters, testing routes, and completing investigations without restarting the run.
